PaddleOCR.dll redistribution status

The PaddleOCRSharp 6.2.0 NuGet package describes PaddleOCR.dll as a
"third-party free community edition" component. The PaddleOCRSharp upstream
license and README state that the Apache-2.0 grant for the C# wrapper does not
grant rights for third-party DLL files and that users/distributors must obtain
the applicable permission from the original rights holder.

Accordingly, MathGao-Gtools records the license for PaddleOCR.dll as
NOASSERTION. This notice is not a license grant and does not resolve whether
the file may be publicly redistributed. A public binary release containing
this DLL must remain blocked until redistribution permission is independently
confirmed, or the component is replaced/omitted.
